ABOUT

Academic Profile

Dr. Bikash Barman is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Guwahati College, Assam. His academic interests include algebraic structures and graph-theoretic approaches to algebra.

He completed his Ph.D. in Mathematics from Cotton University, with research focused on the interdisciplinary study of algebraic structures.
